Receipts

Proof of payment for your clients. Their accounts team usually needs one for every invoice they pay.

1Client pays through PayPalPayPal emails them a receipt by itself. Nothing for you to do.
2Client pays another wayBank transfer, cash or cheque, recorded with Mark paid. Nobody sends them proof, so it shows here as To send.
3You send it in one clickPayPilot numbers it (R-1035), emails a private link, and shows when the client opened it.
