One place for the money side of your work

Type "Bill Marcus for the pitch deck, $1,200, due in 15 days", or paste a screenshot of the client's message. PayPilot drafts a real PayPal invoice, warns you if the client usually pays late, and suggests a deposit or an early-pay discount.

Every unpaid invoice gets a plan: a friendly heads-up, then firm, then final with an optional late fee. The AI writes each email on the right day, warm for a good client and formal for a repeat late payer, and waits for your OK.

Attach the final files to the invoice. Your client gets a page that stays locked until PayPal confirms the payment, then opens by itself. No more sending the work and hoping.

"Can I buy a $1,800 laptop on the 21st?" PayPilot plays out 4,000 possible futures of who pays you and when, after PayPal fees, tax and rent, and answers with the chance, the most you can safely spend, and the invoice that decides it.

PayPilot learns from every paid invoice: which clients are slipping away, which industries pay best and fastest, which work is growing, who should be on a retainer, and where to focus next quarter.

Up and running in a minute

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

  1. 01Connect PayPal

    Paste your PayPal app keys, or start with realistic demo data for your profession.

  2. 02Describe the work

    In plain words or a screenshot. PayPilot drafts the invoice and plans the follow-ups.

  3. 03Approve and get paid

    You approve each step. PayPilot watches PayPal, notifies you, and keeps your books current.

The AI proposes. You decide.Every invoice, reminder, late fee and cancellation is a draft until you click approve. Writes to PayPal are idempotent, so nothing is sent twice.

How it's built

Serious engineering,
open source.

PayPal Invoicing v2Create, send, remind, record payments, late fees and discounts through full updates, cancel, import. Webhooks verified by signature.
Agentic AIGemini tool calling across 13 typed tools, with vision for screenshots. Claude and OpenAI-compatible models also work.
Real statisticsPer-client payment models with empirical Bayes, a 4,000-path Monte Carlo cash forecast, RFM customer segments. The AI explains numbers; it never invents them.
Built to lastNode.js, Express 5 and SQLite. A pending-action queue for every write, live updates over Server-Sent Events, and an end-to-end test suite in CI.
