Up and running in a minute

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

  1. 01Tell it who you are

    Freelancer or registered business, and what you sell. Connect PayPal, or look around with demo data first.

  2. 02Describe the work

    In plain words or a screenshot. PayPilot drafts the invoice and plans the follow-ups.

  3. 03Approve and get paid

    You approve each step. PayPilot watches PayPal, notifies you, and keeps your books current.

The AI proposes. You decide.Every invoice, reminder, late fee and cancellation is a draft until you click approve. Writes to PayPal are idempotent, so nothing is sent twice.

How it's built

Serious engineering
under a calm interface.

PayPal Invoicing v2Create, send, remind, record payments, late fees and discounts through full updates, cancel, import. Webhooks verified by signature.
Agentic AIGemini tool calling across 13 typed tools, with vision for screenshots. Claude and OpenAI-compatible models also work.
Real statisticsPer-client payment models with empirical Bayes, a 4,000-path Monte Carlo cash forecast, RFM customer segments. The AI explains numbers; it never invents them.
Built to lastNode.js, Express 5 and SQLite. A pending-action queue for every write, live updates over Server-Sent Events, and an end-to-end test suite in CI.
